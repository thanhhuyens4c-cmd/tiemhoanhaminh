## Brand & Style

This design system translates the intimate warmth, tactile authenticity, and poetic storytelling of an artisanal neighborhood flower studio into a clean editorial e-commerce experience. The design captures the feeling of unwrapping fresh blossoms hand-tied in natural fibrous kraft paper and tied with simple twine.

The visual style blends **Handmade Warmth** with **Clean Editorial E-Commerce**:
- **Tactile Paper Surfaces:** Warm cream canvas layers mimic unbleached craft paper, providing an organic, soothing foundation that lets botanical photography shine.
- **Storytelling & Emotional Restraint:** Expressive hand-lettered moments inspire emotional connection, while structured catalog grids, clear information architecture, and grounded earthy typography ensure effortless shopping.
- **Natural Crayon Accents:** Playful, delicate floral crayon motifs appear strictly as framing devices and section ornaments, never creating visual noise.
- **Strict Anti-Patterns:** Pure pitch black (`#000000`), aggressive neon drop shadows, corporate gray borders, and cold glassmorphism are explicitly avoided.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from the physical floral brand emblem—pairing unbleached fibrous paper tones with living foliage greens, romantic rose hues, and soft wildflower accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#3E9B61` Leaf Green):** Represents fresh stems and foliage. Governs high-intent commercial conversion actions ("Thêm vào giỏ hàng", "Tiến hành thanh toán", positive validation).
- **Secondary (`#D95A82` Brand Pink):** The signature floral rose hue from the logo. Drives emotional engagement, collection discovery banners, hero CTAs ("Khám phá hoa"), and cart badge counters.
- **Tertiary (`#FAF7E9` Warm Cream):** Provides structural surface grouping for alternating sections and warm card panels.
- **Neutral (`#4B4240` Earth Charcoal):** Replaces harsh corporate black with a rich espresso-charcoal, ensuring optimal readability across all device displays while preserving emotional warmth.

### Functional Roles & Accents
- **Canvas Tones:** `#F3EED8` (Paper Cream) acts as the base brand canvas, while `#FFFFFF` ensures crisp product card cutouts and contrast behind photography.
- **Floral Accents:** `#43B7D0` (Flower Blue), `#A45AA7` (Flower Purple), `#F4C52D` (Sun Yellow), and `#F29A38` (Flower Orange) appear strictly as delicate status indicators, star ratings, and subtle floral illustrations. They should never overwhelm the core green and cream foundation.
- **Proportional Distribution:** 60% Paper Cream / White, 15% Leaf & Dark Green, 12% Brand Pink, 5% Soft Pink, 4% Blue & Purple, 4% Sun Yellow & Orange.

## Typography

The typographic system adheres to a strict 75/25 ratio between crisp clarity and handcrafted intimacy:

1. **Brand & Emotional Script (`Pacifico`):**
   - Allocated ~25% of visual prominence.
   - Strictly reserved for hero headlines, poetic quotations, greeting card previews, and editorial chapter breaks.
   - **Prohibited:** Never use `Pacifico` for paragraphs, forms, prices, tables, or navigation.

2. **System & E-Commerce Sans (`Be Vietnam Pro`):**
   - Handles ~75% of the interface.
   - Exceptional native rendering for Vietnamese tonal markers (`ê`, `ơ`, `ư`, `ả`, `õ`).
   - Line heights for body copy are set to 1.6–1.65 to ensure diacritic accents remain unobstructed and legible.
   - Prices always use SemiBold (`600`) in `#4B4240` or `#277A4D` with the standard `₫` currency symbol.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point base grid system combined with an editorial container max-width of `1240px` (outer boundary `1280px`).

### Breakpoints & Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (`≥ 1200px`):**
  - Standard container: `1240px` centered with auto margins.
  - Product Catalog: 4-column grid with `20px–24px` gutter.
  - Checkout Layout: 2-column split (60% shipping and gift notes, 40% order summary).
  - Contact Page: 3-column asymmetric layout (workshop details, studio map, florist inquiry).
- **Tablet (`768px – 1199px`):**
  - Product Catalog: 2 to 3-column grid with `16px–20px` gutter.
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):**
  - Product Catalog: 2-column layout with `12px–14px` gutter.
  - Detail, Cart, and Checkout flows collapse to a clean single column.
  - Primary call-to-actions expand to full viewport width (`100%`).

### Vertical Rhythm
Section spacing provides ample breathing room to evoke an unhurried, artisanal atmosphere. Desktop sections feature `80px–100px` vertical margins, while mobile displays scale to `48px–64px`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm paper tonal layering, subtle surface contrast, and soft hairline borders rather than cold synthetic drop shadows:

- **Tonal Canvas Stacking:** Depth is created by layering surfaces. Base canvas is `#F3EED8` (Paper Cream), secondary grouped containers use `#FAF7E9` (Warm Cream), and focused interactive elements or product cards sit on clean `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
- **Subtle Organic Shadows:** Where elevation is necessary for layering (such as sticky headers, quick-view modals, and mobile sheets), soft, extra-diffused warm tints are used:
  - *Card Resting:* `0 2px 8px rgba(75, 66, 64, 0.04)`
  - *Card Hover:* `0 8px 20px rgba(75, 66, 64, 0.08)`
  - *Modal / Drawer:* `0 16px 36px rgba(41, 72, 60, 0.12)`
- **Hairline Framing:** Crisp 1px strokes in `#E5DDCE` and `#E5DED5` delineate components on light backgrounds, preventing the interface from dissolving into low-contrast mush.
- **Global Sticky Header:** Header remains fixed at `top: 0` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` over a 95% opaque `#F3EED8` ground.

## Shapes

The design system uses balanced, organic corner curves that feel soft and friendly while maintaining architectural structure:

- **Buttons & Interactive Controls:** Fixed between `8px` and `12px`. Full pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are strictly prohibited for buttons to avoid looking like a generic template.
- **Product & Content Cards:** Standardized at `12px` (acceptable tolerance `10px–14px`).
- **Product Photography:** Strict `1:1` square aspect ratio with an internal radius of `8px` and `overflow: hidden`.
- **Form Inputs & Quantity Selectors:** Standardized at `8px` corner radius.
- **Badges & Tag Chips:** Softly rounded at `6px`.

  - Radius: `10px` | Padding: `12px 24px` (Desktop), `14px 20px` (Mobile)
  - Hover: `#277A4D` with slight lift; Active: `#29483C` with `scale(0.98)`
- **Brand Storytelling Button ("Khám phá hoa", "Bộ sưu tập mới"):**
  - Surface: `#D95A82` (Brand Pink) | Text: `#FFFFFF`
  - Hover: `#C83F70` (Deep Pink)
- **Secondary Outline Button ("Xem chi tiết"):**
  - Surface: Transparent | Border: `1px solid #29483C` | Text: `#29483C`
  - Hover: Background `rgba(41, 72, 60, 0.06)`, border-color `#277A4D`
- **Ghost / Text Button ("Xem tất cả →"):**
  - Surface: Transparent | Text: `#D95A82` or `#277A4D`
  - Hover: Underline with 4px offset

### 2. Product Card
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` | Border: `1px solid #E5DED5` | Radius: `12px`
- Padding: `14px 14px 18px 14px`
- Media: `1:1` square image container, `8px` radius. On hover, image scales up subtly (`scale(1.04)`, transition `240ms ease`) revealing a Quick-View button and floating heart wishlist icon.
- Content Stack: Bouquet Title (`Be Vietnam Pro` 500, `#4B4240`), category label (`#857B76`), price (`#277A4D` SemiBold), and compact leaf-green "Thêm +" action button.

### 3. Inputs & Form Fields
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` | Border: `1px solid #E5DDCE` | Radius: `8px`
- Height: `46px` (desktop), `48px` (mobile touch compliance)
- Text: `#4B4240` | Placeholder: `#857B76`
- Focus State: Border `1.5px solid #3E9B61` with subtle focus ring `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(62, 155, 97, 0.15)`.
- Handwritten Card Note Field: A specialized textarea styled with `#F3EED8` background, providing a live script preview rendered in `Pacifico`.

### 4. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base: `1px solid #E5DDCE` with `4px` radius for checkboxes; circular for radios.
- Checked State: Filled with `#3E9B61` with crisp white check/dot icon.

### 5. Quantity Stepper
- Layout: Horizontal capsule containing `[-]`, `Count`, `[+]`.
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` | Border: `1px solid #E5DDCE` | Radius: `8px`
- Buttons: Minimum `36x36px` touch area with `#FAF7E9` hover background.

### 6. Badges & Product Chips
- **"Hoa tươi trong ngày":** Soft Pink `#F3A8C0` surface, Deep Pink `#C83F70` text, `6px` radius.
- **"Mới về":** Leaf Green `#3E9B61` surface, Pure White text.
- **"Bán chạy":** Sun Yellow `#F4C52D` surface, Earth Charcoal `#4B4240` text.

### 7. Global Navigation & Utilities
- Header: Sticky Paper Cream `#F3EED8` bar with 1px border `#E5DDCE`.
- Navigation Links: SemiBold `#4B4240`; active item receives a Deep Leaf `#277A4D` indicator bar.
- Utility Triggers: Heart/Wishlist, Account Profile, and Shopping Cart featuring a Brand Pink `#D95A82` badge with white count text.
